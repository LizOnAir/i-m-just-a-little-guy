# I‘m just a little guy

A little guy who love to explore. One day, while exploring a nearby cave, he drop into a cave, where consists of many mobs who love to eat humans.

Although he is too small to feed to their appetite, still he is being hunt by them. Try your best to help little guy avoid them and survive as long as possible!

## How to play
Use W/A/D/S or arrow keys to move.
